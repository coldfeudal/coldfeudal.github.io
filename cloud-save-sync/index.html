Cloud Save Sync

A mod for Project Zomboid (Build 42) that backs up single-player saves and keeps them in sync between your computers.

Play on your PC, continue on your laptop, come back to the PC: the mod keeps the save the same on all of them. Every save is stored as a zip archive in a place you choose, so it is also a backup you can go back to.

What it does

  • After you leave a world, the save is compressed and uploaded. Quit to desktop pauses, saves, uploads and closes the game.
  • Autosaves while playing, by default every 30 minutes of unpaused time.
  • At game start, newer versions are downloaded, including saves this computer does not have yet.
  • If the same save was changed on two computers, the mod asks which version to keep.

Where saves can go

  • Google Drive: you sign in through the browser. The mod uses the drive.file permission, so it can see only the files it created itself (folder "Cloud Save Sync") and nothing else in your Drive.
  • Yandex.Disk: you sign in through the browser. The mod can see only its own app folder.
  • A local or network folder, your own SFTP server, or your own FTP / FTPS server.

Your data

Your saves go only to your own account or server. The mod has no server of its own, sends nothing to the author and collects no statistics. Details: Privacy Policy.

Get it

The mod is published on the Steam Workshop for Project Zomboid under the name "Cloud Save Sync - Backup saves to cloud". It requires the ZombieBuddy mod.
