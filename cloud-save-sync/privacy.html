Cloud Save Sync

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Cloud Save Sync ("the mod") is a mod for the game Project Zomboid. It runs only on your computer and copies your single-player saves to a storage location you choose. This policy explains what data the mod accesses and what it does with it.

Summary

  • The mod has no server. The author never receives your saves, your account data or anything else.
  • No analytics, no telemetry, no ads.
  • Everything the mod stores stays on your computer or in the storage you chose.

Data the mod accesses

Google Drive

When you choose Google Drive and sign in, the mod asks for the drive.file permission. With it the mod can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.

  • Files: the mod creates a folder "Cloud Save Sync" and stores in it zip archives of your saves and a small file index.json listing the versions. It reads, updates and deletes only these files: old versions are deleted when they exceed the number you set.
  • Account information: the mod reads your Google account's e-mail address, display name and a stable account identifier (via the Drive "about" request). The e-mail is shown in the mod's settings window so you can see which account is signed in. The identifier lets the mod tell two accounts apart.

Yandex.Disk

When you choose Yandex.Disk and sign in, the mod gets access only to its own app folder on your Disk ("Apps/Cloud Save Sync") and to basic Disk information: your login and account identifier, used the same way as above.

Folder, SFTP, FTP / FTPS

For these options the mod connects only to the folder or server you enter. The server address, user name and password stay on your computer.

How data is stored

  • Sign-in tokens, passwords, the account name and identifier are stored on your computer in Zomboid/CloudSaveSync/config.json. On Windows, tokens and passwords are encrypted for your Windows user (Windows DPAPI).
  • The mod writes its log lines to the game's console.txt. They contain no account names, e-mail addresses, passwords or tokens.
  • Your saves are stored only in the storage you chose: your Google Drive, your Yandex.Disk, your folder or your server.

Sharing

The mod does not share, sell or transfer your data to anyone. Data obtained from Google or Yandex is used only to show you which account is signed in and to store, find and download your save archives.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Cloud Save Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

  • Sign out in the mod's settings window removes the tokens and account name from your computer.
  • To revoke the mod's access to your account: Google - myaccount.google.com/permissions; Yandex - the app access list in your Yandex ID settings.
  • Save archives: delete the "Cloud Save Sync" folder in your Google Drive or "Apps/Cloud Save Sync" on your Yandex.Disk.
  • Local data: delete the folder Zomboid/CloudSaveSync on your computer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
